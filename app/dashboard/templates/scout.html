Why forward CLV, not raw PnL

Every stat on this page carries its own sample size (n) - nothing here is trusted below its minimum. Forward CLV, not reported PnL, is the arbiter of every promotion and demotion: PnL is gameable (luck, cherry-picked near-certain bets, or hiding losers as never-closed "zombie" positions), while CLV measures whether the market actually moved a wallet's way after it acted - independent of whether any one bet has resolved yet. A WATCHLIST wallet is "promising" - one out-of-sample window in, not yet trusted. Only VALIDATED means "verified, repeatedly, out of sample" - confirmed across {{ scout_validation_confirmations }} consecutive forward windows, each requiring a 95% confidence interval whose LOWER bound clears zero, not just a positive average that could be a lucky streak.
