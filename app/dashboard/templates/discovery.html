This screen is historical win rate within a niche only - it does not yet run the market-maker/bot-detection heuristics the global Scout pipeline uses (app/optimization/market_maker.py). A wallet with an unusually high resolved_n relative to how little of the market universe has been walked so far is more likely a high-frequency bot or liquidity provider than a genuine specialist - treat a large n here as a flag to check, not a stronger signal. Forward CLV tracking (the same out-of-sample bar the Scout page's copy list requires) is what actually verifies a niche candidate; a bare CANDIDATE row here is unverified, exactly like a CANDIDATE on the Scout page.
